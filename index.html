Facilities we provide

Everything you need for treatment and recovery, under one roof.

Top-level doctors

Our senior consultants and specialists bring years of hands-on experience across every major field. Each patient gets an unhurried consultation, a clear explanation of the problem and a treatment plan that is personal, practical and kind.

Modern equipment

From digital X-ray, CT and MRI to a fully equipped in-house laboratory, our technology is chosen to give accurate answers fast. Regular upkeep and trained technicians mean reliable results, so treatment can begin without delay.

Patient care

Comfortable private and shared rooms, round-the-clock nursing and clean, quiet surroundings help patients rest and heal. Our staff keep families informed at every step, because a calm family helps a patient recover better.

Quality healthcare

We follow proven clinical protocols, strict infection control and regular safety audits. Every test, medicine and procedure is double-checked, so you receive safe, consistent and effective treatment from admission to discharge.

Medical counseling

Understanding a diagnosis can feel overwhelming. Our trained counselors sit with patients and families to explain the condition, discuss treatment choices, plan diet and recovery, and offer support long after you go home.

Emergency help

Our emergency unit is open day and night with ambulances, a trauma team and an ICU on standby. Patients are assessed within minutes, stabilised quickly and moved to the right specialist without losing precious time.
